Identity
- Product: FIM (Frontier Intelligence Model) by Nort Labs.
- Style: bold, flat, neo-brutalist. Thick shapes, rounded corners, hard offset shadows with no blur. Playful but confident. Innovative. Out of 2035. AGI

Colors (use only these)
- Electric blue #233dff: primary. Buttons, key highlights, logo tile.
- Violet #5e17eb: secondary accent. Gradients with blue, badges, hover states.
- Black #000000: default page background.
- Deep purple #330c81: panels, cards, section backgrounds, shadows on black.
- White #ffffff: text on dark backgrounds, light panels.
No neon glows, no other hues. No generic ai website flaws. Gradients only between blue and violet.

Typography (already loaded, reference by name)
- 'High Tide Geometric': big headlines, logo-style text. Uppercase, short.
- 'Lazy Dog': playful accents. Labels, badges, small taglines. Uppercase.
- 'Geom': body text, paragraphs, buttons, tables.
- Fallback: system-ui, sans-serif.

Components
- Cards and buttons: border-radius 16-24px, flat fill, hard shadow such as 8px 8px 0 #000. On black backgrounds use #330c81 for the shadow so it stays visible.
- No blurred shadows, no thin outlines, no glassmorphism.
- No overglowed neons

Logo
- Use the logo files listed in RESOURCES. Never redraw or recolor them.
- Always place a logo inside a white (#ffffff) panel with padding, because the files are made for light backgrounds.
